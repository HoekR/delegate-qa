## Correction history

### Phase 1 — Initial pattern matching (≤ November 2024)

The occurrence rows were generated by a fuzzy pattern-matching algorithm that assigned each name occurrence in the resolutions to a delegate identity. The algorithm worked well for straightforward cases but had two systematic weaknesses:

1. **Generational confusion** — noble families where the same surname (and sometimes nearly the same given name) recurred across two or three generations. The algorithm frequently collapsed all occurrences under one generation.
2. **Post-1795 title shift** — before 1795 delegates were addressed as *heer* + name; after the Batavian Revolution the form changed to *burger* + name (or name alone). The pattern matcher was not tuned for this shift, producing a high rate of mismatches for sessions from 1795 onward.

Estimated lifespan-violation rate at this stage: **~22 %** of rows with datable delegates (Nov 2024 baseline, ~337 K rows).

### Phase 2 — Bulk review via `supicious_patterns_corrected.csv` (early 2026)

A systematic review of all pattern clusters flagged as suspicious produced `supicious_patterns_corrected.csv`. Each row was manually classified as one of: *merge*, *rename*, *split*, or *flag*. This drove the bulk population of:

- `staged_corrections.json` (75,980 entries) — merges and renames
- `staged_splits.json` (423 entries) — single-row reassignments to new person ids

Effect: the violation rate dropped from ~20 % (April 2025, 755 K rows) to ~13 % (January 2026, 430 K rows). The corrections were very clean: a before/after comparison shows **35,485 violations fixed** and only **20 new violations introduced**.

### Phase 3 — Source verification against resolutions (2026)

For equivocal cases, occurrence years were compared against the actual nomination resolutions in the States General archive (via [app.goetgevonden.nl](https://app.goetgevonden.nl)). Verified resolution URNs were stored in the `resolutie_refs` field of `uq_delegates_baked_20260423.parquet`.

| Metric | Value |
|---|---:|
| Delegates with `resolutie_refs` | 176 / 826 |
| Format | `urn:republic:inv-<inv>-date-<date>-session-<n>-resolution-<n>` |

These references pin a delegate to a specific session date, providing ground-truth constraints for the surrounding occurrence rows.

Verified corrections from this phase were promoted to `approved_corrections.json` (22,607 entries, 39 unique source ids).

### Phase 4 — Manual app corrections (March–April 2026)

Remaining cluster-level errors were corrected individually in the review app. Row-level corrections are stored in `corrections.json` (345 entries as of April 2026). The baked parquet is the frozen checkpoint after all tiers are applied; new corrections accumulate on top until the next bake.

At the current bake the dataset contains 364,003 rows of which 325,927 are measurable (delegate has a known birth or death year). Reducing violations to ≤1 % of measurable rows requires fixing approximately **23,000 more rows**, achievable by:

1. Splitting the top ~10 mixed clusters (accounts for ~12,000 rows).
